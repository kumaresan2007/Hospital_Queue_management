How Queue Works (FIFO)

Essential DSA concept for Viva & Demo

1
FIFO Principle:

First In, First Out — The patient who registers first is served first. Just like a real hospital queue!

2
Enqueue Operation (Add to Rear):

When you click Register Patient, queue.push(patient) inserts the new record at the rear/end.

3
Dequeue Operation (Remove from Front):

When you click Serve Next, queue.shift() extracts the patient at the front/index 0.

4
Front Pointer:

queue[0] points to the patient highlighted with light blue in the table below.

Array Method: push() & shift() Front: O(1) peek No Reordering Allowed
